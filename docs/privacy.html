Privacy policy

Fidget, by Rai Industries. Effective October 9, 2026.

The short version: Fidget collects nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app never connects to the internet, so nothing you do in it leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

Fidget saves a few things on your device so they're there next time:

  • your settings, such as intensity, Discreet mode and favorite toys
  • options you pick for each toy
  • haptic patterns you record in the Haptics Lab
  • the fidget boards you build

We can't see any of it. It's deleted when you delete the app. Like other app data, it can be included in your iPhone's own backups.

Motion

Shake and Snow globe read your phone's motion sensor while they're open, to move things on screen. The readings are used right away and never stored or sent anywhere.

What Apple shares with us

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us crash reports and overall usage numbers. These come from Apple, not from Fidget, and don't tell us who you are.

Children

Fidget doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
