Fidget

Haptic toys for restless hands. By Rai Industries.

Fidget is a set of toys you can feel: things to pick and peel, click, spin, squish and shake, each with its own vibration. It's free, has no ads or accounts, and works offline.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or an idea for a new toy? Email industriesofrai@gmail.com.

Help

I can't feel anything

  • Check that System Haptics is on in Settings › Sounds & Haptics.
  • Turn up Intensity in Fidget's settings, and in the toy's own settings (the gear on its screen).

Open a toy from the Action Button, Control Center or Lock Screen

Every toy has a link. In the Shortcuts app, make a new shortcut with the Open URLs action and the toy's link, then add the shortcut to the Action Button, Control Center or the Lock Screen.

Using it in public

Turn on Discreet mode in settings. It dims toys to near-black and keeps the phone from locking while a toy is open.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Fidget collects nothing. See the privacy policy.

Is Fidget a treatment for skin picking?

No. Fidget gives restless hands something else to do, but it isn't a medical treatment. If picking, pulling or other repetitive habits are hurting you, a doctor or therapist can help. The TLC Foundation for BFRBs has information and can help you find a therapist.
